# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 10, 2025_

**SpotTransfer** uses Google OAuth to authenticate users. We access your Google account only to retrieve your YouTube Music playlists to perform the transfer from Spotify.

We do **not** store your personal information, Google account details, or any playlists on our servers. All actions happen in real-time and data is discarded after processing.

We do **not** share your data with any third parties.

If you wish to revoke access, you can do so via your [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
